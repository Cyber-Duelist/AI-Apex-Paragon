About PersonaDoc AI

PersonaDoc AI is an advanced private Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system. It uses cutting-edge vector embeddings to give large language models a precise memory of your uploaded documents, allowing you to quickly query PDFs, Text files, and more with exact citations.

How to Use

1. Setup: First, ensure your API Key is entered. The app will use this to connect to the models.

2. Uploading: Drag & drop your PDF documents or click the dotted area in the top right to upload. The engine will instantly read and index them.

3. Models: Next to the chat bar, select your preferred AI model. Llama 3.3 70B is the most capable.

4. Targeted Search: Select a specific document from the dropdown to restrict the AI to only read that file.

5. Privacy: Click the trash icon next to any document in the sidebar to securely purge it from the server.
